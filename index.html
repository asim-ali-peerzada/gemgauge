About

What is GemGauge?

GemGauge is a free Gemini usage tracker browser extension for Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi. It adds a live quota ring to Google Gemini’s prompt bar, so you can see how much of your 5‑hour session limit and 7‑day weekly limit you have used before you hit the cap.

Instead of a separate popup or dashboard, the Gemini quota meter sits exactly where you type. It shows a countdown to each reset, turns amber at 70% and red at 90%, and sends quota requests straight to Gemini — no account, no analytics, and no third-party servers.
